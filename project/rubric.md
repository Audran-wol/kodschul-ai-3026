# Pitch Jury: Scoring Rubric

The jury lead uses these rules to score every idea. The total score is out of 10.

## Four criteria, each worth 0 to 2.5 points

| Criterion   | 0 points                          | 2.5 points                                         |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Problem     | Nobody has this problem           | A clear, painful problem that many people have     |
| Customer    | The customer juror would not pay  | The customer juror would pay today                 |
| Feasibility | The engineer juror says it cannot be built | The engineer juror says a small team can build it in 3 months |
| Money       | The investor juror sees no way to earn money | The investor juror sees a clear way to earn money |

## Rules

- Add the four numbers. Round the total to a whole number.
- If two jurors are clearly negative, the total cannot be higher than 5.
- If the idea is illegal or harmful, the score is 0. Say why in one sentence.
- A score of 9 or 10 is rare. Give it only when all three jurors are clearly positive.
- Always give exactly one piece of advice: the single change that would raise the score most.
