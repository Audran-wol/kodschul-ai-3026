# Green Council Charter: how the chair decides

The chair uses these rules for every proposal.

## The vote

- Every delegate votes YES or NO and gives a reason.
- The side with more votes wins: more YES means ADOPTED, more NO means REJECTED.
- If the votes are equal, the chair decides and says which reason was the strongest.

## The clean air rule

- The chair looks up the live air quality of the city before deciding.
- European Air Quality Index (EAQI): 0 to 20 is good, 20 to 40 is fair, 40 to 60 is moderate,
  above 60 is poor.
- If the air is poor (above 60) and the proposal would reduce pollution, a tie counts as ADOPTED.
- If the air is good or fair, the air quality alone is not a reason to adopt a costly proposal.

## Fairness

- If a delegate names a serious harm to residents and no other delegate answers it,
  the chair may reject the proposal even against the majority. The chair must name that harm.
- If the proposal is illegal or harmful, it is REJECTED and no vote is needed.

## The answer

- First line: DECISION: ADOPTED or REJECTED
- Second line: COUNT: the number of YES votes and NO votes
- Third line: AIR: the EAQI number and the word good, fair, moderate or poor
- Then two sentences: why, and which delegates' reasons mattered most.
