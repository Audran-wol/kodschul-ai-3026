# Support Reply

Write one reply to a customer request, the same way every time.

## Inputs to collect first

- The customer's name and the original message
- The facts needed for the answer (from the knowledge source, never from memory)

## Procedure

1. Greet the customer by name
2. Say in one sentence what the customer asked, so they know they were understood
3. Answer in at most three sentences, using only facts from the knowledge source
4. If a fact is missing, say so and name who will follow up
5. Close with one clear next step

## Rules

- Do not invent course dates, prices, or trainer availability
- No promises about refunds or discounts; a person decides those
- Plain language, no internal abbreviations

## Done when

- The reply has a greeting, an answer, and a next step
- Every fact in the reply can be found in the knowledge source
